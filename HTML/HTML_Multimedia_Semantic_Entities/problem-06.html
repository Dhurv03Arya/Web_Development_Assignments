Our Restaurant Story & Executive Chef Profile

A Heritage of Culinary Excellence

Founded in 2012, Savoria ™ has redefined fine dining by merging classic Mediterranean traditions with contemporary Asian gastronomy. Our mission is centered around sustainable sourcing — using 100% organic, farm-to-table ingredients supplied by local farmers. At Savoria ®, every dish represents a harmonious balance of texture, aroma, and vibrant flavor.

"Food is not merely sustenance; it is an emotional voyage. Our kitchen crafts stories served on a plate."

— Chef Antoine Laurent

Over the years, Savoria © has received multiple Michelin Stars and accolades across the culinary world. Whether you visit us for an intimate anniversary dinner or order our gourmet dishes directly to your home, we guarantee an exceptional gastronomic journey.

Meet Executive Chef Antoine Laurent

Chef Antoine Laurent brings over 20 years of international experience across Paris, Tokyo, and New York. Trained at the prestigious Le Cordon Bleu, Chef Laurent specializes in molecular gastronomy and artisanal wood-fired cooking.

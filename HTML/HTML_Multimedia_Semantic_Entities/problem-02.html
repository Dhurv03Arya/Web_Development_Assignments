Hiring Stages

  1. Initial resume review and technical screening call.
  2. Take-home assessment or live technical pairing session.
  3. Panel interviews covering architectural design and core values.

Recruitment Definitions

Screening
An introductory call with a recruiter to align goals, expectations, and basic requirements.
Technical Round
An in-depth evaluation of coding ability, problem-solving skills, and domain knowledge.
HR Round
A conversation dedicated to assessing cultural add, collaboration skills, and compensation.
Offer
The final formal proposal outlining terms of employment, benefits, and start date.

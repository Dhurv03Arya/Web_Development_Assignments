Preperation Tips

  • Bring multiple hard copies of your updated resume along with a digital PDF version
  • Prepare a 30-second elevator pitch summarizing your background, skills, and goals.
  • Dress in business professional or smart casual attire
  • Research participating companies beforehand to ask relevant and insightful questions.
  • Follow up with contacts made during the event via professional platforms within 48 hours.

Attendee journey

  1. Register online and receive your official digital event access pass.
  2. Check in at the venue reception desk to collect your delegate badge.
  3. Attend the inauguration ceremony and opening keynote presentation in Hall A.
  4. Explore company stalls, present your resume, and engage in quick interviews.

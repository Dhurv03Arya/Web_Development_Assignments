Admission Overview

Welcome to Apex Horizon University. We are dedicated to providing a transformative educational experience that combines rigorous academics with practical, real-world application. Our diverse undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs prepare students to excel as innovative leaders in an evolving global landscape.

"Our mission is to foster an inclusive academic environment that empowers students through high-quality education, pioneering research, and dedicated service." — Office of Admission

ELIGIBLITY CRITERIA

To qualify for admission, candidates must satisfy both academic performance thresholds and general proficiency requirements:

Undergraduate Programs
Successful completion of Higher Secondary Schooling (Class 12 or equivalent) with a minimum aggregate score of 60% from a recognized educational board.
Postgraduate Programs
A recognized Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 out of 4.0 (or equivalent).
Language proficiency
All non-native English speakers must submit valid TOEFL or IELTS scores unless prior instruction was completed entirely in English.

APPLICATION PROCESS

  1. Create an Account: Register on the official university portal using a valid email address.
  2. Complete the Application Form: Fill in all personal details, academic history, and program choices accurately.

  3. Upload Supporting Documents: Attach clear, scanned copies of all required certificates and identification.

  4. Pay Application Fee: Submit the non-refundable processing fee through the online payment gateway.

  5. Final Submission: Review your application carefully and submit it prior to the cutoff date.

  6. Track Application Status: Monitor your portal dashboard for document verification updates and potential interview calls.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Prepare scanned copies of the following items before beginning your online application (order of submission does not matter):

  • Official Academic Transcripts (High School / University Mark Sheets)
  • Secondary School Certificate (Proof of Age)
  • Valid Government-Issued Photo ID or Passport
  • Recent Passport-Sized Photographs
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP) detailing academic and career goals
  • Two Letters of Recommendation (Academic or Professional)
  • Standardized Test Scores SAT/GRE/GMAT, if applicable

Important Terms & Definitions

  • Eligibility: The set of minimum academic and administrative qualifications a candidate must satisfy to apply for a degree program.

  • Merit List: The official ranked list of applicants generated based on academic scores, entrance test results, or evaluation metrics.

  • Counselling: An interactive seat-allocation session where candidates verify original documents and lock in their preferred major or branch.

  • Scholarship: Financial assistance awarded to qualifying candidates based on academic excellence, sports achievements, or demonstrated financial need.

  • Registration: The official administrative process of paying tuition fees and enrolling in classes to confirm your admission status.

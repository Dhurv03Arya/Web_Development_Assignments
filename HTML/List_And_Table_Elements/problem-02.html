The Golden Hearth

Welcome to The Golden Hearth, where traditional wood-fired culinary techniques meet modern artisanal flavors. We take pride in sourcing local, seasonal ingredients to craft unforgettable dining experiences for you and your family.

Menu Categories

Starters

  • Trattoria Garlic Bread: Freshly baked sourdough brushed with roasted garlic butter and herbs

  • Wild Mushroom Arancini: Crisp risotto balls stuffed with wild mushrooms and melted fontina cheese.

Main Course

  • Wood-Fired Ribeye Steak: 12oz Prime ribeye grilled over oak wood, served with rosemary compound butter.

  • Tuscan Truffle Pasta: Handcrafted fettuccine tossed in a rich black truffle and aged parmesan cream sauce.
  • Garden Harvest Bowl: Roasted quinoa, grilled asparagus, fresh avocado, and a lemon-tahini dressing.

Desserts

  • Classic Tiramisu: Espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with light mascarpone cream and cocoa powder.
  • Artisanal Molten Lava Cake: Warm chocolate cake with a molten center, paired with house-made vanilla bean gelato.

Beverages

  • Signature Citrus Craft Soda: Sparkling house-made soda infused with fresh blood orange and basil.
  • Estate Red Blend Wine: A full-bodied local red wine featuring rich dark berry and subtle oak notes.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

For the ultimate multi-course culinary journey, follow this curated sequence:

  1. Course 1: Begin with the Wild Mushroom Arancini to awaken your palate with warm, savory flavors.
  2. Course 2: Sip our Signature Citrus Craft Soda as a crisp, refreshing mid-course palate cleanser.
  3. Course 3: Indulge in the Wood-Fired Ribeye Steak as your centerpiece main course.
  4. Course 4: Conclude your meal with the Classic Tiramisu paired with a double shot of espresso.

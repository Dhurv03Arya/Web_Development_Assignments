Pre-Practical Class Sequence

Students must strictly execute the following steps in sequence prior to entering any computer or hardware laboratory session:

  1. Store all personal bags and non-essential items in the designated locker area outside the lab.
  2. Sign the lab attendance register located at the laboratory entrance door.
  3. Locate your assigned system workbench as per your roll number index.
  4. Inspect all hardware components (keyboard, mouse, monitor) for physical defects before booting up.
  5. Log into your authorized university network account and open the assigned practical worksheet.

Classroom Rules

All enrolled students are required to maintain strict adherence to classroom decorum:

  • Maintain silence and discipline inside lecture halls, tutorial rooms, and common corridors.
  • Keep mobile phones on silent or switched off during active teaching periods.
  • Eating, drinking (except water), and chewing gum are strictly prohibited inside classrooms.
  • 75% minimum attendance is mandatory in every course to qualify for mid-term and final examinations.
  • Respect departmental property; report any damaged equipment or furniture immediately to faculty.

Academic Terminology & Definitions

Lecture
A classroom session dedicated to explicit theoretical instruction delivered by the course instructor.
Lab
A practical hands-on session where students execute programming tasks, experiments, and simulations.
Tutorial
A small-group interactive session focused on problem-solving, doubts clarification, and homework discussion.
Internal Assessment
The continuous evaluation process comprising class tests, assignments, quizzes, and lab performance throughout the semester.
Practical
An applied examination assessment where students demonstrate hands-on implementation and problem-solving skills.

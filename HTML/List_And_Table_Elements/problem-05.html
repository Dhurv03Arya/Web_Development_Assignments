Registration Process

  1. Visit the official festival registration portal.
  2. Create a primary team leader or individual account using a valid college email address.
  3. Select your target competition categories and individual sub-events.
  4. Fill in complete participant credentials (Full Name, Student ID, College Name, Phone Number).
  5. Pay the respective event registration fee through the integrated online gateway.
  6. Download your generated Registration Pass containing your unique QR-code ticket
  7. Present the digital ticket along with your physical college ID card at the festival entrance desk on event day.

Fest Rules and Regulations

  • Carrying a valid physical College ID Card and Registration Pass is mandatory at all times inside the campus premises.
  • Alcohol, tobacco, illegal substances, and hazardous materials are strictly prohibited.
  • Participants must report to their assigned competition venues at least 30 minutes prior to the scheduled start time.
  • Decisions made by event judges and faculty coordinators are absolute and non-negotiable.
  • Damaging university property will lead to immediate disqualification of the entire team and financial recovery penalties.

Key Terminology & Definitions

  • Open Event: A competition segment accessible to all enrolled university students regardless of their academic stream or major.
  • Team Event A competitive contest that requires a minimum designated group size (e.g., 2–6 members) to register and perform together.
  • Individual Event:A single-person competition evaluating individual skill, performance, or knowledge
  • Registration:The formal administrative procedure of submitting participant details and paying prescribed fees to confirm entry.
  • Coordinator:A designated faculty member or senior student leader responsible for overseeing event management, scoring, and logistics.
  • Final ROund: The ultimate stage of a multi-stage competition where top shortlisted qualifiers compete directly for podium prizes.

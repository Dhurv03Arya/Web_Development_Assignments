Understanding Credit Scores & Responsible Borrowing

Your credit score reflects your financial reliability. Maintaining a score above 750 ensures lower interest rates and faster loan approvals. Pay credit card balances in full each month to avoid heavy interest charges.

Investment Basics: Compounding Interest

Starting early allows your investment to grow exponentially over time due to compound growth. The formula $A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$ demonstrates how small, consistent contributions yield long-term wealth.

Core Financial Terminology

Principal
The original sum of money lent or invested, excluding interest or capital gains. Example: ₹5,00,000 baseline loan amount.
Interest Rate
The percentage of principal charged by the lender for the use of assets, typically calculated annually (e.g., 8.5% p.a. ±0.5% floating rate adjustment).
EMI
Equated Monthly Installment — a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month.
Tenure
The fixed period of time over which a loan must be repaid or an investment is held to maturity (ranging from 12 months to 30 years ∞).

Essential Financial Tips

  • Build an emergency fund covering at least 6 months of living expenses.
  • Follow the 50/30/20 budgeting rule: 50% Needs, 30% Wants, and 20% Savings.
  • Never share your OTP, PIN, or passwords with anyone — bank officials will never ask for them.
  • Diversify your asset portfolio across equity, debt, and liquid instruments.

Steps to Open a Savings Account

  1. Gather mandatory identity and address proofs (Aadhaar, PAN Card, Passport size photos).
  2. Fill out the digital or paper account application form accurately.
  3. Complete your In-Person Verification (IPV) or Video-KYC process.
  4. Deposit the initial minimum opening balance amount required by the institution.
  5. Receive your welcome kit, debit card, and activate online/mobile banking access.

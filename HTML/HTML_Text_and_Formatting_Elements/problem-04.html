MATHEMATICS FORMULAE SHEET


ALGEBRA

Square of a Sum: (a+b)2=a2 + b2+2ab
Square of a Difference: (a-b)2=a2 + b2-2ab
Cube of a Sum: (a+b)3=a3 + b3+3ab(a+b)
Cube of a Difference: (a-b)3=a3 - b3-3ab(a-b)
Difference of Squares: a2-b2=(a−b)(a+b)

GEOMETRY

Area of Triangle = 1/2 * base * height
Area of Rectangle = length * width
Area of Circle = π * r2
Volume of Sphere = 4/3 * π * r3
Volume of Cylinder = π * r2 * height

MENSURATION


Surface Area of Sphere = 4 * π * r2
Surface Area of Cylinder = 2 * π * r * (r + height)
Area of Trrapezium = 1/2 * (height)*(base1+base2)
Area of Parallelogram = base * height
Area of Rhombus = 1/2 * (diagonal1 * diagonal2)

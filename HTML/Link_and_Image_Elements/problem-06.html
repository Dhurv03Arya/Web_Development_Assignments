About TechFest

Welcome to TechFest 2026! This is our annual college technology festival hosted by the student committee. It is a big 3-day event where students from different colleges come together to show their projects, take part in coding contests, and win prizes.

If you like computers, robots, or learning new technology things, you should definitely attend this event with your friends!

BIG ANNOUNCEMENT: The 1st place winner of the Hackathon gets a $5,000 cash prize!

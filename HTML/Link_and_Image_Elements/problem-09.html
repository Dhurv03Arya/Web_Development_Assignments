About Us

Welcome to The Rameshwaram Cafe! Founded with a vision to deliver authentic, premium-quality South Indian tiffin delicacies, we bring traditional recipes from South Indian households into a fast-paced, hygienic quick-service environment.

Our commitment is simple: uncompromised quality, rich fresh ghee, traditional spices, and steaming hot dishes served fresh from the pan straight to your plate every single day.

Signature Items

Ghee Podi Dosa:Our flagship dish! Crispy on the outside, soft on the inside, generously coated with home-style podi (gunpowder spice mix) and drenched in pure melted ghee.

Ghee Button Idli: Soft, melt-in-your-mouth mini idlis floating in hot sambar and topped with aromatic podi and clarified butter.

Filter Coffee: Authentic South Indian filter coffee brewed fresh with rich chicory blend and frothy whole milk.

Defining terms

Consultation
The initial formal meeting between a patient and a healthcare provider to discuss medical history, evaluate current symptoms, order diagnostic tests, and establish a preliminary diagnosis or treatment plan.6+
Follow Up
A subsequent medical visit or review intended to monitor a patient's progress, evaluate the effectiveness of a prescribed treatment or medication, review test results, and adjust care plans as necessary.
OPD
A hospital or clinic division that provides diagnosis, care, and treatment to ambulatory patients who do not require an overnight admission or extended stay in a hospital ward
Appointment

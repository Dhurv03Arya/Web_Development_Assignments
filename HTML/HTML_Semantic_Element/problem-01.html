Key Admissions Terminology

Important terms and concepts prospective candidates should understand before applying:

Eligibility Criteria
The minimum aggregate percentage and compulsory subject requirements in qualifying examinations (10+2 or Graduation).
GLAET (GLA Entrance Test)
The online entrance examination conducted by GLA University for admission into engineering and professional courses.
Merit List
Ranked shortlist prepared based on candidate scores in qualifying exams and performance in GLAET entrance rounds.
Scholarship
Financial assistance grants awarded based on 10+2 board exam percentages, JEE Main ranks, or GLAET merit scores.
Application Fee
A mandatory non-refundable registration fee of ₹1,000 for evaluating application credentials.

How to Apply (Step-by-Step)

  1. Register on this official portal and create your applicant login credentials.
  2. Fill out your personal information, local address, and prior academic marks in the application form below.
  3. Select your preferred course and chosen schedule slot for the GLAET entrance test.
  4. Pay the online application registration fee.
  5. Track your registration and admit card status using your application reference format: 
    GLA-2026-NOIDA-XXXXX

Scholarships & Financial Support

GLA University offers merit-based fee waivers to deserving candidates:

  • Merit Scholarship for B.Tech: Up to ₹33,000/year waiver for students scoring above 90% in 10+2.
  • JEE Main Merit Waiver: Direct fee concessions for high percentile rankers in JEE Main.
  • BCA / BBA Merit Grants: Discounts for high achievers in qualifying board exams.

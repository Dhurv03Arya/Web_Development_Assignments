About UNiversity

GLA University Greater Noida Campus functions as an off-campus, industry-integrated learning hub of GLA University. Designed specifically to bridge academia with the corporate world, this modern campus leverages its strategic location in National Capital Region (NCR) to offer students early corporate exposure, industry mentorship, and practical skill acquisition.


Operating with approvals from regulatory bodies including UGC and AICTE, the Greater Noida campus emphasizes hands-on problem-solving, project-based evaluation, and tech-driven education. Powered by partnerships with industry giants like Google and Google Cloud, it serves as a pioneer for agentic AI implementation and contemporary technical training.

Academic Programs

The academic curriculum at GLA University spans diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral (Ph.D.) levels. Programs are designed around flexible credit systems, industry partnerships, and project-based learning models.

Key Degrees Offered

  • Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science & Engineering
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Financial Management & Marketing
  • Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) and Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm)
  • Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) and Master of Computer Applications (MCA)
  • Integrated BA LL.B. / BBA LL.B. Law Degrees
  • Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) across Engineering, Sciences, and Management

Campus Facilities

The campus is developed to support academic study alongside extracurricular growth, physical wellbeing, and student life. It features modern architecture and digital connectivity across all premises.

Facility Highlights

  • Central Library: Houses over 150,000 physical volumes, subscriptions to IEEE, Scopus, and Springer journals, along with quiet e-learning pods.
  • Specialized Research & Automation Centers: Equipped with Rockwell Automation, CNC machinery, 3D printing labs, and automotive design workshops.
  • Residential Hostels: Separate housing facilities for male and female students with air-conditioned options, 24/7 security, and mess services.
  • Sports & Gymnasium Arena: Includes grounds for cricket, football, basketball, badminton courts, and indoor sports facilities.

Student life

Life at GLA Greater Noida goes far beyond classroom lectures. Our vibrant campus ecosystem ensures all-round personal growth:

  1. Clubs & Societies:Cultural, coding, robotics, drama, and sports clubs managed entirely by students.
  2. Annual Tech & Cultural Fest: Inter-college hackathons, live music nights, and sports tournaments held every year.
  3. Campus Facilities: High-speed Wi-Fi, modern library, air-conditioned cafeteria, and indoor/outdoor sports arenas.

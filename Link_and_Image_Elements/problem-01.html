About UNiversity

GLA University Greater Noida Campus functions as an off-campus, industry-integrated learning hub of GLA University. Designed specifically to bridge academia with the corporate world, this modern campus leverages its strategic location in National Capital Region (NCR) to offer students early corporate exposure, industry mentorship, and practical skill acquisition.


Operating with approvals from regulatory bodies including UGC and AICTE, the Greater Noida campus emphasizes hands-on problem-solving, project-based evaluation, and tech-driven education. Powered by partnerships with industry giants like Google and Google Cloud, it serves as a pioneer for agentic AI implementation and contemporary technical training.

Departments

GLA University houses various specialized departments designed to foster innovation, industry integration, and hands-on learning across disciplines. Each department is equipped with modern laboratories and led by qualified academic faculty.

Core Faculties and Schools


Institute of Engineering & Technology: Covers core engineering domains including Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering.
Institute of Business Management: Focuses on corporate administration, digital marketing, finance, and international business studies.
Institute of Pharmaceutical Research: Provides accredited education in pharmaceutical sciences and medicinal chemistry.
Department of Applied Sciences & Humanities: Offers foundational education in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Professional Communication.
Institute of Legal Studies & Research: Delivers integrated programs bridging corporate law, constitutional law, and legal advocacy.

Academic Progress

The academic curriculum at GLA University spans diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral (Ph.D.) levels. Programs are designed around flexible credit systems, industry partnerships, and project-based learning models.

Campus Facilities

The campus is developed to support academic study alongside extracurricular growth, physical wellbeing, and student life. It features modern architecture and digital connectivity across all premises.

Facility Highlights


Central Library: Houses over 150,000 physical volumes, subscriptions to IEEE, Scopus, and Springer journals, along with quiet e-learning pods.
Specialized Research & Automation Centers: Equipped with Rockwell Automation, CNC machinery, 3D printing labs, and automotive design workshops
Residential Hostels: Separate housing facilities for male and female students with air-conditioned options, 24/7 security, and mess services.
Sports & Gymnasium Arena: Includes grounds for cricket, football, basketball, badminton courts, and indoor sports facilities.

Important Information

Admissions to GLA University are conducted primarily through the university's entrance assessment test (GLAET) as well as national examinations such as JEE Main, CUET, and CAT/MAT depending on the chosen program.
For engineering and computer applications students preparing for placement training, the university provides coding assessments. Here is an example of a fundamental database query pattern covered in the technical skill development module: 

SELECT student_id, first_name, last_name, gpa
FROM student_records
WHERE department = 'Computer Science' 
  AND gpa >= 3.5
ORDER BY gpa DESC;
Students must maintain the minimum attendance requirements and follow the university code of conduct outlined during orientation programs every academic session.
